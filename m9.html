Facts from Arup and the Royal Academy of Engineering (Ingenia): the bridge opened on 10 June 2000 and closed two days later; about 90,000 people crossed on the first day, up to 2,000 at once; measured sideways frequencies were about 0.49 Hz (centre span), 0.77 Hz (south span) and 1.05 Hz (north span); sideways crowd loading is possible below about 1.3 Hz; 37 viscous dampers and 52 tuned mass dampers were fitted for about £5 million, and the bridge reopened on 22 February 2002. In this model the three spans are added into one sensor record, and the sway amplitudes are made-up teaching values. It is a teaching model, not Arup's analysis.
