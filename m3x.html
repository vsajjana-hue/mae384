Nozzle Exit Mach

The problem. A rocket engine's bell-shaped nozzle speeds the exhaust up past the speed of sound. Designers pick the exit area; the exhaust Mach number M follows from the area–Mach relation below. You cannot solve it for M with algebra, and it has two answers: one subsonic, one supersonic. A rocket needs the supersonic one. Find it before the test-stand countdown.
