Facts from the investigation by the National Bureau of Standards (NBS BSS 143, 1982) and later accounts: the collapse happened at about 7:05 p.m. on July 17, 1981, during a tea dance; 114 people died and more than 200 were injured. The original design met only about 60% of the Kansas City building code minimum, and the as-built fourth-floor connection could carry only about 30% of the load the code required of it. The forces here are representative per-connection values chosen to match those ratios (dead load 30 kN per walkway, about 0.12 kN per person at this connection, connection strength 62 kN); they are not the investigation's measured loads.
