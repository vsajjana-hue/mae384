Stick the Landing

The problem. A rocket booster is falling toward a landing pad at 900 km/h. Its engine is too powerful to hover, so it gets one burn: light it too late and it hits the pad; light it too early and it stops in mid-air, then drops. Before lighting the engine, the flight computer must predict the whole burn by solving an ODE, and it has very little computing time. You choose its method and step size.
