Facts from NASA's Orbiter Thermal Protection System fact sheet: tile surfaces up to 1,260 °C (2,300 °F); LI-900 silica tiles at 144 kg/m³ (9 lb/ft³); an aluminium airframe that softens above about 175 °C (350 °F); tiles 2.5 to 12.7 cm thick. This model uses one representative diffusivity (α = 6×10⁻⁷ m²/s), an assumed surface-temperature history, and an insulated back face, the worst case. It is a teaching model, not the orbiter's design analysis.
