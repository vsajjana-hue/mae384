What is being solved? A 260 × 110 grid of cells is advanced in time with an explicit method (lattice Boltzmann), about 300 time steps per second. Nothing is pre-recorded. The same ideas from MAE 384 apply: a step size, a stability limit, and an error that shrinks with a finer grid.
